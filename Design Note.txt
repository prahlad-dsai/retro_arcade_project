Design Note
Domain
Retro-Arcade Smart Token & Cabinet Management Network
The system manages an modern arcade gaming center. Gamers have to maintain their respective token balance that are used to join virtual queues for arcade cabinets. When a gamer  joins a queue a certain amount of tokens are automatically deducted. Arcade cabinets maintain high scores, and players who beat existing records earn reward tickets. The system also tracks queue activity, machine utilization, revenue generated from token spending, and the gamer who currently holds the most high-score crowns.

5. System Rules
Rule-1: If the gamer doesn't have the required amount of token balance then response is "409 Conflict" with reason "Insuficient balance of tokens".
Rule-2: A gamer may have only ONE active session else(Prevents queue hoarding).
Rule-3: The high scores can updated only if the new score is greater than formal high score.
Rule-4: A cabinet cannot be joined if it's status is under maintenance.
Rule-5: A queued session can only be moved to playing status if the cabinet is operational
